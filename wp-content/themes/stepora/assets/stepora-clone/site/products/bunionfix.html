    How Does It Work?

    The Stepora™ Bunion Fix administers advanced alignment therapy. It's designed to support the realignment of the big toe and gradually alleviate inflammation by using a powerful patented hinge joint mechanism.

    It helps release muscular tension by simply pulling your big toe back to its natural position, resulting in pain-free natural realignment of your toe joint over time.

    This releases years of built up tension, straightens and shrinks the bunion, reduces pain, and prevents further growth to get you back on your feet to stand tall and confident again.
